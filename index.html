Frequently asked questions

How do I file a claim?

Call our 24/7 claims line at 1-800-555-0199 or use the contact form above and select "File or track a claim." Have your policy number ready to speed things up.

How can I check the status of an existing claim?

You can check claim status by calling our claims line or emailing support with your claim number in the subject line. Status updates are also sent automatically by email at each major step.

How do I update my billing or payment information?

Select "Billing & payments" in the contact form, or call our general support line during business hours. Have your policy number and updated payment details ready.

Can I make changes to my policy online?

Most policy changes — like updating your address, adding a driver, or adjusting coverage limits — can be requested through this contact form or by phone. A specialist will confirm the change with you before it takes effect.

What information should I have ready when I contact support?

Your policy number, the email or phone number on your account, and a brief description of what you need help with. This helps us resolve your request faster.
